About Me

I am Christian Calle, a Ph.D. student originally from Celica, Loja, Ecuador. Since March 2024, I have been part of the Doctoral Consortium of Valparaíso, a collaborative program between UTFSM, PUCV, and UV. My doctoral research is conducted under the supervision of Patricio Guzmán and Hugo Parada.

Contact: christiancalle141@gmail.com
